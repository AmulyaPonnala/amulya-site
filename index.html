// what two startups taught me
01

Pivot fast.

Loyalty to an idea that will not pay is not grit. It is ego. I move the moment the money says move.

02

Talk first, build second.

I did not ship Luna before speaking to people across the world about it. Validation comes before code now, not after.

03

The gap was never the tech.

It was distribution. Two founders who both wrote code, and no one who sold. That was the whole problem.

He codes. I market.

My cofounder can build anything in tech. I make sure the world hears about it. We stopped being two engineers with no seller. That layer is removed. Tech and distribution, on purpose.
